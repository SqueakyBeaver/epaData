How these numbers are calculated

Download CSV (all categories) Download Excel
  1. Emissions in short tons × {{ short_ton_kg }} = kilograms.
  2. Kilograms × the TRACI factor for the pollutant and category = potential impact. Pollutants are added up only within the same category; categories are never added together because their units differ.
  3. Impact per MWh = total impact ÷ total gross load, using only the unit-years that have both a result and a positive gross load.

A unit-year with a missing amount for a pollutant that the category needs has no result for that category (counted under "unit-years missing"); it is never counted as zero. These are potential impacts for comparing groups. They do not measure actual illness, local exposure or proven environmental damage.
