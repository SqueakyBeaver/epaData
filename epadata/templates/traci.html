TRACI Characterization Factors

EPA's TRACI gives, for each pollutant, how much one kilogram contributes to each impact category. The factors stored here are multiplied by CAMPD emission masses to get potential impacts.

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

How the calculation works

  1. CAMPD reports short tons. Convert to kilograms: mass_kg = mass_short_tons × {{ short_ton_kg }}
  2. For each impact category c: impact_c = Σ (mass_kg of pollutant i × factor of i for c)
  3. Optionally per MWh: impact intensity_c = impact_c / gross load (MWh)

Each category keeps its own unit (for example kg CO2 eq); categories are never added together. Results are potential impacts for comparison, not measurements of actual harm.
