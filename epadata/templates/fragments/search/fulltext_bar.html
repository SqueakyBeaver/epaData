Full-text search

Search everything saved in the database, both uploaded files and data retrieved from the EPA. Type keywords (a facility, county, state, fuel or year) or write a whole sentence.

In a sentence it finds the fuel, state, year, an amount limit on CO2, SO2, NOx, heat input, gross load or operating hours (greater than, less than, at least, between), and "top" or "bottom" N. A line above the results shows how it read the sentence. Anything else is matched as words, best match first. A record with no value for an amount never matches a condition on it. This is separate from the retrieval filters above.

For exact searches use field:value, for example state:KY or year:[2020 TO 2025]. Fields: facility_name, state, county, unit_id, facility_id, unit_type, primary_fuel, secondary_fuel, source_category, program_code, controls, year, dataset_name, co2_mass, so2_mass, nox_mass, heat_input, gross_load, operating_time. Put a phrase in quotes, add * to finish a word, or use OR and NOT.
