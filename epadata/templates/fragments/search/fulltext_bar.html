Full-text search

Searches everything saved in the database: uploaded files and data retrieved from the EPA. Type a few keywords (a facility, county, state, fuel or year) or a whole question, for example coal-fired units in Kentucky in 2025 with CO₂ greater than 500,000 short tons or top 10 facilities by CO₂.

In a sentence it picks out the fuel, state, year or years, an amount limit on CO₂, SO₂, NOx, heat input, gross load or operating hours (greater than, less than, at least, between), and top or bottom N. It shows how it read your sentence. Everything else is matched as words, best match first. A record with no value for an amount never matches a condition on that amount. This search is separate from the retrieval filters above.
