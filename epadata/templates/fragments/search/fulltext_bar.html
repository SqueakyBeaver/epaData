Full-text search

Type a question in your own words, or just a few keywords. This searches everything saved in the database (uploaded files and data retrieved from the EPA), best match first. It is separate from the retrieval filters below: it does not use them, and they do not use it.

How does this search work?

Sentences. The search looks for these in what you type and shows you how it read them (“Understood as…”):

  • Fuel: coal, natural gas, oil, diesel, petroleum coke, wood (“coal-fired” works).
  • State: the full name (Kentucky) or the capital-letter code (KY).
  • Year: 2025, a range (2022-2024, between 2022 and 2024) or since / before / after a year.
  • Amounts: CO₂, SO₂, NOx, heat input, gross load or operating hours, with greater than, more than, over, at least, less than, under, at most or between (“500,000”, “500k” and “2 million” are all fine).
  • Anything else (a facility name, a county) is matched as ordinary words.

Keywords. Each saved record (one unit in one year) is indexed by the words in its facility name, state, county, unit ID, unit type, fuels, source category, program, control equipment, year and dataset name. Searches ignore capital letters and word endings (plant also finds plants). A match in the facility name counts more than one in the dataset name. If nothing matches, the words are retried as word beginnings. A record with no value saved for an amount (for example no CO₂) can never match a condition on that amount.
