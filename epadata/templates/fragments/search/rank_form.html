Rank units or facilities

Find the highest or lowest values of one measurement in everything saved in the database. Units are ranked one by one for each year. Facilities add their units together, separately for each year, so give a year if you want each facility to appear only once.

Records with no value for the measurement are left out, not counted as zero. If a unit and year was saved more than once, only the newest is used. The full-text search on the EPA Retrieval page understands sentences like "top 10 facilities by CO2 in 2025" too.
