Rank units or facilities

Find the highest or lowest for one measurement: the 10 facilities with the most CO₂, the 20 units with the highest gross load, the 5 units with the lowest SO₂. It searches everything saved in the database.

Units are ranked one by one for each year. Facilities add their units together, separately for each year, so pick a year if you want each facility only once. The filters narrow the records before ranking and match part of the text. Records with no value for the measurement are left out (never counted as zero), and the result tells you how many. If a unit and year was saved more than once, only the newest is used. You can also type a sentence like top 10 facilities by CO₂ in 2025 in the full-text search on the EPA Retrieval page.
